How Taptico's fidgets actually feel

Taptico is not a sound pack. Each toy is a haptic pattern on the Taptic Engine — short, metallic, and meant to stay in the hand so a shared desk stays quiet.

The three that stay free

Combination lock. You roll the dial through detents. Each tick is a small, even click — closer to a padlock than a game spinner. Good when you want a loop with a little precision.

Toggle switches. These are the heavy ones. The snap is sharp and short, like a real rocker hitting its stop. If you like a decisive click, start here.

Magnetic slider. Softer than the toggles. You drag, it catches, it lets go. Less of a “hit” and more of a pull — useful when the lock and switches feel like too much attack.

What the lifetime set adds

The rest of the desk is behind a one-time unlock: a cube you can turn in the hand, a spinner, a zipper with tooth-by-tooth catches, a pop-it, and more of the same idea — metal-feeling toys, not cartoon boings.

None of this is a campaign of levels. You open a toy, fidget, close it. No ads, no account.

What it is not

  • Not a speaker demo. If you need loud ASMR in headphones, this is the wrong product.
  • Not on Android yet. Coming soon. The iPhone patterns use the Taptic Engine.
  • Not a subscription drip. Three toys free; the others are a lifetime unlock.
