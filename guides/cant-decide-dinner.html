Guide · Food

Can't decide dinner? Cook it or go out.

Staring at the fridge and staring at a map are different problems. One app spins a meal you can cook. The other picks a nearby restaurant when nobody wants to choose. Use one. Don’t mash them together.

Pick the job, then pick the app

If the food is going to come out of your kitchen, you need a recipe — diet, time, allergies, and steps. That’s What’s 4 Dinner.

If the food is going to come from down the street, you need a place — ratings, directions, or delivery — and you need it fast. That’s Fork It.

Cook at home

What's 4 Dinner

Spin it. Cook it. Love it.

When you’d use this

You have ingredients, or you can shop. You want a full recipe, not a restaurant name. Filters cover food type, diet, cook time, and allergies. Save favorites and keep a Culinary Passport streak if you like that kind of loop.

What it is not

It does not find a table nearby. It will not navigate you to a diner. If the decision is “where are we eating out,” skip this one and use Fork It.

Go out

Fork It

Can't decide? Say Fork It.

When you’d use this

Nobody wants to pick. You’re already hungry. Pick a vibe, tap Fork It, and take the nearby spot it lands on — ratings, directions, or delivery — or spin again if it’s not the one.

What it is not

It is not a cookbook. It will not give you ingredients and steps. Fork It is Android-only. If you want a recipe on iPhone, that’s What's 4 Dinner.
