20-Minute Black Bean Tacos (vegetarian)

Spiced black beans, crunchy cabbage, and lime in a warm tortilla. Vegetarian, and on the table in about twenty minutes.

20 min Serves 2 Vegetarian

Ingredients

Recipe serves 2.

  • 15 oz canblack beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • 1/2small onion, diced
  • 2 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • 1/2 tspchili powder
  • 1/2 tspkosher salt
  • 2 tsptomato paste, or 1/4 cup salsa
  • 6small corn or flour tortillas
  • 2 cupsshredded cabbage or lettuce
  • 1lime, in wedges
  • optionalcheese, yogurt, cilantro, hot sauce

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Warm the oil over medium heat. Cook the onion about 3 minutes, until soft.

  2. Step 2

    Stir in the garlic, cumin, and chili powder for 30 seconds.

  3. Step 3

    Add the beans, salt, and tomato paste. Mash about half the beans so the filling holds. Splash in water if the pan looks dry. Simmer 4 minutes.

  4. Step 4

    Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet.

  5. Step 5

    Fill with beans and cabbage. Finish with lime, and cheese or yogurt if you want them.

  6. Step 6

    Taste the beans before you fill. They should be salty enough for the cabbage.

Vegetarian as written. Skip the cheese and it is vegan. Corn tortillas keep it gluten-free when the salsa is, too.
