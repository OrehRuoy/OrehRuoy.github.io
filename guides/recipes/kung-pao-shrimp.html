25-Minute Kung Pao Shrimp Stir-Fry

Plump shrimp, zucchini, and crunchy peanuts in a sweet, spicy sauce. Takeout flavor at home, in about twenty-five minutes.

25 min Serves 3 Shrimp Stir-fry

Ingredients

Recipe serves 3.

  • 1 lbraw shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1zucchini, cut in half-moons
  • 1red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 cuproasted peanuts
  • 3scallions, sliced
  • 2 tbspneutral oil
  • 3 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, minced
  • 2 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tbsprice vinegar
  • 1 tbsphoney
  • 1 tspchili garlic sauce, or 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tspcornstarch
  • 2 tbspwater
  • to tastekosher salt
  • to servecooked rice

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Pat the shrimp dry and season with a pinch of salt. Stir the soy sauce, vinegar, honey, chili, cornstarch, and water together.

  2. Step 2

    Start the rice if you are serving it.

  3. Step 3

    Heat the oil in a wide skillet over high. Cook the shrimp in one layer, about 1 minute per side, until just pink. Move them to a plate.

  4. Step 4

    Add the zucchini and pepper. Stir-fry about 2 minutes, until the edges color.

  5. Step 5

    Add the garlic and ginger. Stir 20 seconds, until it smells sweet.

  6. Step 6

    Pour in the sauce. When it turns glossy, return the shrimp, the peanuts, and most of the scallions. Toss 30 seconds.

  7. Step 7

    Serve over rice and finish with the rest of the scallions.

Peanuts and shellfish. If either is off the table, cook the ground beef tacos instead.
