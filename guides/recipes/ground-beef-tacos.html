25-Minute Ground Beef Tacos

Seasoned ground beef in warm flour tortillas, with lettuce, tomato, cheddar, and sour cream. Build-your-own taco night, about twenty-five minutes.

25 min Serves 4 Beef Tacos

Ingredients

Recipe serves 4.

  • 1 lbground beef
  • 1 smallonion, diced
  • 2 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tbspchili powder
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • 1/2 tsppaprika
  • 1/2 tspkosher salt
  • 1/4 tspblack pepper
  • 1/3 cupwater
  • 8flour tortillas
  • 2 cupsshredded lettuce
  • 1tomato, diced
  • 1 cupshredded cheddar
  • 1/2 cupsour cream

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Warm a skillet over medium-high. Cook the beef and onion, breaking the beef up, until the beef is browned and the onion is soft. Pour off extra fat if the pan is swimming.

  2. Step 2

    Stir in the garlic, chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper. Add the water and simmer 2 minutes, until the beef looks saucy.

  3. Step 3

    Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet, or wrapped in a towel in the microwave, until they bend without cracking.

  4. Step 4

    Fill each tortilla with beef, lettuce, tomato, cheddar, and a spoon of sour cream.

Warm tortillas tear less. If they crack, ten seconds in a dry skillet softens them.
