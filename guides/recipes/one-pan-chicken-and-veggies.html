One-Pan Lemon Chicken and Vegetables

Lemon-roasted chicken thighs with broccoli and peppers, all on one pan. Dinner for four, or two with lunch tomorrow.

35 min Serves 4 Sheet pan

Ingredients

Recipe serves 4.

  • 1 1/2 lbboneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1lemon, sliced thin
  • 3 cupsbroccoli florets
  • 1red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 smallred onion, in wedges
  • 3 tbspolive oil
  • 3 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspkosher salt
  • 1/2 tspblack pepper
  • 1 tsppaprika
  • 1 tspdried oregano

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).

  2. Step 2

    Toss the chicken, broccoli, pepper, and onion with the oil, garlic, salt, pepper, paprika, and oregano.

  3. Step 3

    Spread in one layer on a sheet pan. Tuck the lemon slices between the pieces.

  4. Step 4

    Roast 22 to 26 minutes, until the chicken is 165°F (74°C) in the center and the broccoli has dark edges.

  5. Step 5

    Rest 2 minutes. Squeeze on more lemon if you want it brighter.

Thighs stay juicy at this heat. Chicken breasts are done closer to 20 minutes, before they dry out.
