35-Minute Sheet-Pan Sausage and Roasted Vegetables

Smoky sausage with broccoli, peppers, and red onion on one pan. Toss it, roast it, and eat.

35 min Serves 4 Sausage Sheet pan

Ingredients

Recipe serves 4.

  • 1 lbsmoked sausage, sliced into coins
  • 3 cupsbroccoli florets
  • 1red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1red onion, sliced
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tspkosher salt
  • 1/2 tspblack pepper
  • 1 tspgarlic powder
  • 1 tspdried Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).

  2. Step 2

    Toss the sausage, broccoli, peppers, and onion with the oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning.

  3. Step 3

    Spread everything in one layer on a sheet pan.

  4. Step 4

    Roast 20 to 25 minutes, until the broccoli has dark edges and the sausage is browned.

  5. Step 5

    Rest 2 minutes. Taste for salt.

Read the sausage label if anyone avoids gluten.
