Games

Circuit Sort

Sort screws. Fix circuits.

A satisfying screw-sorting puzzle about matching colored screws into bins to repair old electronics. 175+ levels, daily challenges, power tools, and a workbench that grows as you progress.

Food

What's 4 Dinner

Spin it. Cook it. Love it.

Stuck on dinner? Spin filters for food type, diet, cook time, and allergies — then get a full recipe with ingredients and steps. Save favorites, track your Culinary Passport streaks, and go Premium for unlimited searches and a weekly meal planner.

Food

Fork It

Can't decide? Say Fork It.

A fast random restaurant picker for nights when nobody wants to choose. Pick a vibe, tap Fork It, and get a nearby spot with ratings, directions, or delivery — then spin again if it's not the one.

Fitness

SensorFit

Hands-free. Auto-counted.

Turn your phone into a hands-free workout counter. Place your device, start a set, and SensorFit uses proximity and accelerometer sensors to count push-ups, squats, sit-ups, and more — no tapping mid-set, no smartwatch required.

Sensory

Spectrum Sync

Your social language coach.

An AI tone coach that helps you write with confidence in texts, chat, social media, and email. Real-time feedback catches blunt or intense phrasing before you hit send — with smart rewrites and privacy-first setup. Built especially for autistic and neurodivergent users, and anyone who overthinks the Send button.

Android — live now

A background coach that watches what you type in the apps you already use — social media, chat, SMS, and email — and flags tone before you hit send. Keep your current keyboard.

iOS — live as a keyboard

On iPhone, Spectrum Sync is a custom keyboard. Switch to it with the globe key in Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, and more. Coaching appears as a pill above the keyboard with rewrite suggestions — same coach, different plumbing.

Sensory

StimPad

Audio sensory fidget. Feel every sound.

A sensory sound tool for neurodivergent users — tap into calm, focus, and sensory joy with a library of stim sounds. Save favorites, explore soothing audio textures, and unlock the full soundboard with StimPad Plus.

Coming soon on the App Store
Sensory

Taptico

Haptic fidget & focus tool.

An ad-free haptic fidget for desk workers and anyone who likes tactile sensory input. Spin a combination lock, snap heavy toggle switches, tilt metallic bearings, play a theremin pad, and type on a mechanical key matrix — all driven by the phone's haptics. Coming soon on iOS.
