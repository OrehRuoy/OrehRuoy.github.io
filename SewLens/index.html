SewLens: Sewing Pattern Maker

SewLens helps home sewists get a sewing pattern drafted to their own measurements.

You enter your body measurements and take or choose a photo of a garment style you like. SewLens suggests a matching sewing design, then drafts a pattern from your measurements that you can print on Letter or A4 paper at home.

No account is needed and there are no ads. Patterns are made-to-measure estimates, so a test garment (muslin) is always a good idea before cutting your good fabric.

The patterns are built on FreeSewing, an open-source pattern project released under the MIT License.
