SewLens: Sewing Pattern Maker

Made-to-measure PDF drafting.

SewLens helps home sewists get a sewing pattern drafted to their own measurements. You enter your body measurements and take or choose a photo of a garment style you like. SewLens suggests a matching sewing design, then drafts a pattern from your measurements that you can print on Letter or A4 paper at home.

Photos are only used to find a design and are not stored. Measurements stay on the phone. No account is needed and there are no ads.

Patterns are made-to-measure estimates, so a test garment (muslin) is a good idea before cutting your good fabric.

The patterns are built on FreeSewing, an open-source pattern project released under the MIT License.

The first pattern is free. After that, buy pattern credits or unlock SewLens Unlimited, a one-time purchase. No subscription. Restore Purchases is in the app.

Available in English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, and Dutch. Requires iOS 15 or later.
