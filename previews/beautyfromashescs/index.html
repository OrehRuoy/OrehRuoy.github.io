You don't have to carry it alone

A safe place to be heard, and practical tools to grow.

Everyone has a story, and sometimes that story includes pain, loss, anxiety, trauma, or feeling stuck. At Beauty From Ashes, we won't only talk through what you've experienced. Together we'll build real skills for emotional regulation, coping, and communication.

You don't need to have everything figured out before you start. Showing up is enough.

Getting started

Three gentle steps

  1. Reach out

    Call, email, or book a free 15-minute consultation to see if we're a good fit.

  2. Check your coverage

    See if your insurance is on the list, or use self-pay or the sliding scale.

  3. Begin at your pace
