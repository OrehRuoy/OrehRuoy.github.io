FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might wonder before reaching out. If your question isn't here, just ask.

What happens in the first session?

Starting therapy can feel vulnerable, so the first session is about getting to know you. We'll talk about what's bringing you in, your history, your strengths, and your goals, plus any questions you have about counseling. Together we'll start shaping a plan that feels meaningful and realistic for you. You don't need to have everything figured out. Showing up is enough.

Is there a free consultation?

Yes. A free 15-minute consultation is a no-pressure way to ask questions and see if we're a good fit.

How do I book?

Call (814) 644-7898 or email beautyfromashescs@gmail.com. Online booking through Headway is coming soon.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Sessions are available by secure video for clients located in Pennsylvania, as well as in person at the Huntingdon office. You'll need a private space and a phone, tablet, or computer with a stable internet connection.

Do you take my insurance?

Lauren accepts many plans through Headway, including Aetna, Cigna, Highmark, Capital Blue Cross, Geisinger, UnitedHealthcare, and several Medicare Advantage plans. Use the insurance checker. Headway confirms your exact coverage when you book.

What if I don't have insurance?

Self-pay rates are $100 per individual session and $110 per couples session, and a sliding scale is available. You also have the right to a Good Faith Estimate.

Do I have to be a Christian?

Not at all. Christian counseling is offered only to clients who ask for it. Everyone is welcome, and your beliefs will be respected either way.

Who do you work with?

Mainly adults, along with some teens, for individual counseling. Couples counseling is also available. If you're unsure whether it's a fit, just reach out.

What are your hours?

Fridays 8 AM–4 PM, and evenings Monday–Thursday. Call or email to find a time that works.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share in counseling is kept confidential, with a few exceptions required by law, such as when someone's safety is at risk. We'll go over these at your first session.

What if I'm in crisis?

Please don't wait for a session. Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7), or call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room if you or someone else is in immediate danger.
