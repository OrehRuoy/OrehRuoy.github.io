About

Meet Lauren Albright

MS, LPC · Licensed Professional Counselor · Founder of Beauty From Ashes Counseling Services, LLC

Hello! I'm Lauren Albright, MS, LPC, a Licensed Professional Counselor with a Master's degree in Clinical Mental Health Counseling. I've spent the past 16 years working in the mental health field, and I work with adults navigating anxiety, trauma, depression, emotional overwhelm, life transitions, relationship stress, neurodivergence, and self-worth challenges.

My approach is compassionate, collaborative, and structured. I believe healing happens when people feel safe, heard, and empowered. In our work together, we won't only process your experiences. We'll also build practical tools to strengthen emotional regulation, coping skills, and communication.

For those who desire it, I also offer Christian counseling, bringing faith and biblical principles into the healing process in a respectful, clinically sound way. It's always your choice.

The heart behind Beauty From Ashes comes from Isaiah 61:3, the promise that restoration is possible even after deep pain. Whatever you're walking through, healing and growth are within reach. If you're ready to begin, I would be honored to walk alongside you.

Lauren
