Care that meets you where you are

Individual and couples counseling, in person in Huntingdon or by secure video anywhere in Pennsylvania.

Adults · some teens

Individual counseling

One-on-one sessions for adults, and some teens, who want to understand themselves better, feel less overwhelmed, and build skills that last. Sessions are structured and evidence-based, with plenty of room for your story.

Teens: I see some teens. Please call or email so we can talk about whether it's a good fit.

Couples counseling

A calm, structured space for partners to communicate more clearly, understand each other's needs, and work through stress and conflict together.

The self-pay rate for couples sessions is $110. See Insurance & Fees.

Always optional

Christian counseling, if you'd like it

For clients who want it, I gently bring Christian faith and biblical principles into counseling alongside evidence-based care. If faith isn't part of your life, or you'd rather keep it out of sessions, that is completely okay. You will be welcomed and respected just as you are.

Just let me know your preference at any point. You can change your mind anytime.
